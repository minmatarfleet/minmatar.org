The alliance would rather you can pay for your own ships than depend on a free-ship fund. The good news is that in faction warfare your first billion ISK takes a few dedicated evenings, not a month.

**Your first billions come from running plexes, not from optimizing the LP store.**

## What plexes pay

Loyalty points (LP) are what faction warfare pays out. The alliance loyalty desk buys Tribal Liberation Force LP. When this guide was written the rate was **700 ISK per LP**. Check the [Loyalty Points](/industry/loyalty/) page for today's rate.

| Activity | LP (frontline) | ISK at 700/LP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small NVY-1 | 22,500 | about 15.8 million |
| Medium NVY-1 | 30,000 | about 21 million |
| Large NVY-1 | 37,500 | about 26 million |
| Battlefield | up to ~150,000 per pilot | about 105 million |

About **60 to 65 solo Small NVY-1 frontline plexes** add up to one billion ISK. LP values change with CCP balance passes. The current table is in [Faction Warfare Complexes](/learning/guides/faction-warfare-plexing/#site-rewards).

## The path

### 1. Plex small and medium frontlines

Fly a cheap Tech I or Navy destroyer or cruiser, find a frontline system, and run Small and Medium NVY plexes. If you cannot read a plex name yet, start with [Faction Warfare Essentials](/learning/guides/onboarding-faction-warfare-essentials/).

### 2. First ~250 million: sell LP to the alliance

Sell your LP on the [Loyalty Points](/industry/loyalty/) desk. At 700 ISK per LP, 250 million ISK is about 360,000 LP.

The desk fills **smaller and newer orders first**. That is deliberate: new players are meant to get paid. You do not need a big stack before you sell.

### 3. First billion: keep plexing

Keep running plexes. Your hourly income is worth far more than squeezing an extra few percent out of each LP.

Once you have some capital, you can try converting LP yourself on high-volume offers. The alliance's conversion coaching starts people on **Navy Cap Booster 3200s**. The [Faction Warfare Loyalty Points](/learning/guides/selling-loyalty-points/) guide walks through it.

## Moving goods

**Do not haul a billion ISK of goods yourself.** Use freight:

- Alliance freight, priced with the [freight calculator](/market/freight/calculator/), for Amamake routes.
- A public freight service for Amamake to Jita and other trade hubs.

## Not week-one plans

These are real, but they are not how you make your first billion:

- **Maximizing the LP store.** It needs capital and market knowledge you do not have yet.
- **Multiboxing for skill extractors.** Guides exist in Discord. It is a long game.
