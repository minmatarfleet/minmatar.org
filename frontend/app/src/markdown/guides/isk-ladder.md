This guide answers one question: **what should I run between fleets to make ISK at my skill level?** It puts every repeatable income method on the same scale so you can compare a solo activity with a group one, and it ends with a progression that pays for skill injectors without turning the game into a job.

## How to read the numbers

- **ISK/h per pilot.** Every figure is what *you* keep per hour. Group activities are already divided by the number of people in the group, so a 3-pilot abyss trio showing 114M means 114M each, not 114M for the three of you.
- **Loyalty points are priced at 700 ISK per LP.** Faction warfare pays in loyalty points (LP), which you turn into ISK on the alliance [loyalty desk](/industry/loyalty/) or in the LP store. 700 is a realistic rate for Tribal Liberation Force LP; see [Faction Warfare Loyalty Points](/learning/guides/selling-loyalty-points/) for how conversion works.
- **Abyssal numbers are net of filaments** and come from the full [abysstracker.com](https://abysstracker.com/) run database (last 12 months, median loot at Jita prices), divided by the pilots in the site, over the median site time plus two minutes to reset.
- **Plexing numbers assume the sites are there.** Frontline systems only spawn so many plexes an hour, and other friendlies are farming them too.
- **Nothing includes ships lost to PvP.** This is a warzone. Fly what you can afford to lose.

Prices are from October 2026 and move with the market. Treat the ranking as stable and the exact numbers as approximate.

## The short version

| If you are... | Run this | ISK/h per pilot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alpha, day one | A Thrasher in Small NVY-1 plexes on a frontline | ~50M |
| Alpha, with two to four friends | ELT sites (Contested Field Research Facility) in destroyers | 63–95M |
| Omega, under 15M SP | A Hawk in T2 Dark abyssals, solo | ~77M |
| Omega, 15M+ SP, three of you | Three Hawks in T4 Dark abyssals, or a Moderate NVY-3 in battlecruisers | ~110M |
| Omega, 20M+ SP, three of you | Two Retributions and a Deacon in T5 Firestorm abyssals (55M hulls) | ~190M |
| Omega, with a billion to spend | A Gila or Ishtar in T6 Gamma abyssals, solo | 380–400M |

Two rules fall out of the data:

- **Below T3, do not group up for abyssals.** A frigate abyssal site burns three filaments and has one loot cache, so three pilots in one site split one pot. The group only clears about 1.5 to 2 times faster, never 3 times, so each of you earns less than running your own site. Group income lives in plexes, where every pilot gets a full payout up to the site's cap.
- **Omega before skill injectors.** A [Daily Alpha Injector](https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Skill_injectors) (about 40M for 50,000 SP) costs roughly 800 ISK per skill point. A Large Skill Injector (about 737M) costs 1,843 ISK per skill point once you pass 5M SP. One hour of destroyer plexing buys the daily injector, and Omega (500 PLEX, about 2.46B a month) is what unlocks every method above 80M/h.

## Faction warfare

Plexes are the capture sites in warzone systems. You warp to one, sit inside the capture radius for 10 or 15 minutes, kill the NPC that spawns, and get paid LP when the timer finishes. Each site's name tells you the ship size it allows and how many pilots get a full payout. [Faction Warfare Basics](/learning/guides/faction-warfare-basics/) explains the map and system types; [Faction Warfare Complexes](/learning/guides/faction-warfare-plexing/) has every site's LP and timer.

**Frontlines only.** Rearguard plexes pay almost nothing and command-ops systems pay two thirds. The [Warzone Report](/warzone/) and the in-game faction warfare map show where the frontlines are this week.

Payouts that are not hourly but matter:

- **Insurgency victory.** When pirates [invade the warzone](https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Insurgency), every militia pilot who helped suppress a winning insurgency gets up to 1.5M LP, about 1.05B, with the maximum at 45% personal suppression and 0.4× on a loss. You earn it by running ADV plexes in the insurgency systems, which is ordinary plexing.
- **Ice Refinery Heist.** An insurgency site paying 150,000 LP per pilot, 5 to 30 pilots, 4-hour respawn. Both sides want it.
- **Battlefields.** 50,000 LP plus freighter loot since June 2026. See [Faction Warfare Battlefields](/learning/guides/faction-warfare-battlefields/).
- **Kill LP.** Every militia kill pays LP based on the hull, split across everyone on grid. The fights pay too.

## Abyssal deadspace

Abyssals are 20-minute PvE pockets you enter with a filament. Three rooms, a loot cache in each, and if the timer runs out you lose the ship and the pod. Frigate sites take three frigates and burn three filaments, destroyer sites two, cruiser sites one; the loot is shared by whoever is in the site. Start with the [Abyssal Deadspace overview](/learning/guides/abyssals/) for deep-safes, loot drop-offs and the security-status table, and the [EVE University abyssal page](https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Abyssal_Deadspace) for room-by-room mechanics. Fits for every hull and tier, with real run data, are on [abysstracker.com/fits](https://abysstracker.com/fits).

## A progression that pays for injectors

1. **Day one, alpha.** Thrasher in Small NVY-1 plexes while fleets are running, Kestrel T0 abyssals when nobody is on. Sell LP on the [loyalty desk](/industry/loyalty/). From day two, buy a Daily Alpha Injector every day; your first hour of plexing pays for it. See [Your First Billion ISK](/learning/guides/onboarding-first-billion/).
2. **Week one.** ELT sites with whoever is online, Hookbill T1 Dark as filler, every fleet you can make. Buy a Republic Fleet Firetail for plex fights and a Hookbill as milestones. Omega in about a month of evenings.
3. **Omega, 5–15M SP.** ADV-1 plexes in a navy destroyer, ELT sites, solo Hawk T2 Dark as PvE. First Large Skill Injector after roughly 10 hours of play. Train toward Assault Frigates V and the [cruiser doctrines](/ships/doctrines/list/).
4. **15–30M SP.** Three Hawks in T4 Dark on quiet nights, Moderates and battlefields with the alliance. A Large Skill Injector every 7 hours of play if you want one.
5. **30M+ SP.** Retribution and Deacon Firestorm trios, then a T6 Gamma Gila. Stop injecting at 50M SP, where a Large Skill Injector drops to 300,000 SP.

Alternate every session: one plexing block, one fleet, at most two abyss runs. End each phase with a ship you wanted, not just an injector.

## Prices used

- Large Skill Injector 737M (500,000 SP under 5M SP, 400,000 up to 50M, 300,000 above). Daily Alpha Injector 40M. PLEX 4.92M, 500 per month for Omega.
- Filaments (each): Dark T4 1.85M, T5 13.98M, T6 46.4M. Firestorm T5 12.36M, T6 25.9M. Gamma T6 28.7M.
- Hulls: Kestrel 0.6M, Thrasher 0.8M, Hookbill 8.9M, Hawk 22.7M, Retribution 25.4M, Deacon 18.4M, Nergal 280M.

Abyss survival rates are from logged runs and under-count deaths. Plexing rates assume uncontested frontline spawns.
