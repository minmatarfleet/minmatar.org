This guide ranks every repeatable way a Minmatar Fleet pilot makes ISK on one scale: **ISK per hour, per pilot**, so a solo activity and a group activity can be compared. Use it to pick what to run between fleets at your skill level, and to plan skill injectors.

Numbers are from October 2026. Loyalty points are priced at 700 ISK/LP, which is realistic for Tribal Liberation Force LP and already hard to beat in volume. Abyssal numbers come from the full abysstracker.com run database (last 12 months, median loot at Jita prices), minus filaments, divided by the pilots in the site, over median site time plus two minutes of turnaround. Nothing here includes ships lost to PvP.

## The short version

| If you are... | Run this | ISK/h per pilot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alpha, day one | Thrasher in a Small NVY-1 on a frontline | ~50M |
| Alpha, with friends | ELT site (Contested Field Research Facility), 3–5 destroyers | 63–95M |
| Omega, under 15M SP | Hawk in T2 Dark abyssals, solo | ~77M |
| Omega, 15M+ SP, three of you | Hawk ×3 in T4 Dark abyssals, or a Moderate NVY-3 in battlecruisers | ~110M |
| Omega, 20M+ SP, three of you | 2 Retribution + Deacon in T5 Firestorm abyssals (55M hulls) | ~190M |
| Omega, a billion to spend | Gila or Ishtar in T6 Gamma abyssals, solo | 380–400M |

Two rules fall out of the data:

- **Below T3, do not fleet up for abyssals.** A frigate site burns three filaments and has one loot cache. Three pilots sharing a T0 or T1 site each earn a third of what three solo sites pay, and the trio only clears about 1.5–2× faster, never 3×. Group content pays in plexes, where every pilot gets a full payout up to the site cap.
- **Omega before Large Skill Injectors.** A Daily Alpha Injector (40M, 50k SP) costs 800 ISK per skill point. A Large Skill Injector (737M) costs 1,843 ISK per skill point once you pass 5M SP. One hour of destroyer plexing buys the daily injector; Omega (2.46B a month in PLEX) is what unlocks every income method above 80M/h.

## Every method

ISK/h is per pilot. Ranges reflect weather, tier, or how many sites spawn.

| ELT site (Contested Field Research Facility) | 3–5 | 63–95M | Any destroyer (alpha) | 45,000 LP per pilot per 10 minutes, 2–3 per hour. The best alpha-accessible rate in the game. Spawn-limited. |
| Moderate NVY-3 | 3 | 92–110M | Navy or T1 battlecruiser, 50–100M | 52,500 LP per pilot per 15 minutes. This is where the fights are. |

| Zohar's Chosen hunting | 1 | 250–750M | 1.5B Loki, Crystal implants | See the [Zohar's Chosen guide](/learning/guides/zohar-hunting/). Corruption-5 systems allow bubbles; the loot pool is shared by every hunter. |
| L5 mission blitz | 1 | ~355M | 1.5B Golem or Barghest, 7.0 standing | See the [L5 guide](/learning/guides/level5-missions/). Low effort once the standing grind is done. |

Not hourly, but real:

- **Insurgency victory.** Every contributor on a winning suppression side gets up to 1.5M LP (about 1.05B), with the maximum at 45% personal suppression and 0.4× on a loss. The contribution is ordinary ADV plexing in insurgency systems.
- **Ice Refinery Heist.** 150,000 LP per pilot per site, 5 to 30 pilots, 4-hour respawn, and the other side wants it too.
- **Battlefields.** 50,000 LP plus freighter blue loot since June 2026.
- **Kill LP.** Militia kills pay LP based on hull, tech level and the victim's rank, split across everyone on grid.

## A progression that does not get boring

1. **Day one, alpha.** Thrasher in Small NVY-1 plexes during fleet hours, Kestrel T0 when nobody is on. Sell LP on the [loyalty desk](/industry/loyalty/). Buy a Daily Alpha Injector every day from day two.
2. **Week one.** ELT groups in whatever destroyers people have, Hookbill T1 Dark as filler, every fleet going. Buy a Firetail for plex fights and a Hookbill as milestones. Omega in about a month of evenings.
3. **Omega, 5–15M SP.** ADV-1 in a navy destroyer, ELT groups, solo Hawk T2 Dark as PvE. First Large Skill Injector after roughly 10 hours.
4. **15–30M SP.** Hawk ×3 T4 Dark on quiet nights, Moderates and battlefields with the alliance. A Large Skill Injector every 7 hours of play if you want one.
5. **30M+ SP.** Retribution and Deacon Firestorm trios, then a T6 Gamma Gila. Stop injecting at 50M SP, where a Large Skill Injector drops to 300k SP.

Alternate every session: one plexing block, one fleet, at most two abyss runs. End each phase with a ship, not just an injector.

## Prices used

- Large Skill Injector 737M (500k SP under 5M SP, 400k to 50M, 300k above). Daily Alpha Injector 40M. PLEX 4.92M, 500 per month.
- Dark filaments: T4 1.85M, T5 13.98M, T6 46.4M each. Firestorm T5 12.36M, T6 25.9M. Gamma T6 28.7M.
- Hulls: Kestrel 0.6M, Thrasher 0.8M, Hookbill 8.9M, Hawk 22.7M, Retribution 25.4M, Deacon 18.4M, Nergal 280M.

Plexing rates assume uncontested frontline spawns; ELT and Moderate spawns cap group income before pilot skill does. Abyss survival rates are of logged runs and under-count deaths.
