Orders are alliance manufacturing requests — the ships and items fleet commanders and market seeders need built. This page is the shared build board, not a personal shopping list.

*   **Browse by location** — Active orders are grouped by where they need to be delivered. Switch locations, search by item name, and open an order for the full material and assignment breakdown.

*   **Claim and build** — Log in, then claim part of an order line for one of your characters. Use the planner from an order item if you want a shopping list and cost estimate before you start jobs.

*   **Coordinators** — On an order, volunteers can cover blueprints, minerals, or PI so producers know who to ping for BPs and feedstock.

*   **Deliver** — When your run is ready, create a delivery contract from your claim, then invoice so you get paid against the order’s target prices.

*   **Summary and history** — Order summary shows expected profit from stored build costs and ask prices. History lists finished orders when you need the paper trail.
